2. Local game data
The game stores progress, mass, upgrades, achievements, language, preferences and remaining bonus time on your device. You do not need a player account.
If you enable the optional local event log, a limited history of gameplay events is stored on your device. This feature does not send the log to us. Disabling it in Settings deletes that log.
We do not upload your save file to a studio-operated backup server. Any backups provided by your operating system depend on its own settings and services.
3. Advertising on Android
The Android edition uses Google AdMob for banner ads, interstitial ads associated with collapses, and optional rewarded ads.
The advertising SDK may process IP addresses and approximate location, advertising or device identifiers, ad interactions, and diagnostic information. These data support advertising delivery, measurement, security and fraud prevention. Google and its advertising partners may receive information depending on the service configuration and your privacy choices.
Further information:

4. Advertising choices
The app uses Google User Messaging Platform to present privacy choices where applicable and checks the consent status before requesting ads.
You can review available choices through Settings → General → Ad privacy. Available options depend on your region and the applicable consent requirements. Declining personalized advertising does not necessarily remove ads or prevent all processing of technical data.
You can also manage your advertising identifier through Android's privacy settings. Rewarded ads are optional and are requested when you choose to use the rewarded-ad button.
5. Optional gameplay analytics
In builds where GameAnalytics is configured and available, sharing gameplay statistics is off by default. It is enabled only when you choose to share statistics in Settings → General.
Events may describe sessions, active play time, progression, unlocks, achievements, tutorial steps and upgrade purchases made with in-game currency, together with the game version and language. The SDK uses a randomized identifier and may process technical device, operating-system, network and approximate country information.
We use these statistics to understand progression and improve the game. Pseudonymous data are not necessarily anonymous. Turning sharing off stops future submissions; it does not automatically delete previously submitted data.
See GameAnalytics privacy information.
6. Support and recipients
If you email us, we process your email address and message to answer your request. Please avoid sending sensitive information or your entire save file unless needed.
Relevant recipients include our email provider, Google and its advertising partners for advertising, and GameAnalytics when optional analytics are enabled and available. We do not directly sell player databases; this statement does not exclude the advertising-related sharing described above.
7. Purposes and legal bases
We process necessary local information to provide the game and retain your preferences. We request consent for optional analytics and for advertising-related processing or device storage where consent is required.
Where applicable, support and security processing relies on our legitimate interests in responding to players and protecting the service. Legal obligations may also require information to be retained. Withdrawing consent does not affect the lawfulness of processing that took place before withdrawal.
8. Retention and deletion
Local progress remains on your device until you reset it or remove the relevant application data. Delete save, in the main menu, resets gameplay progress and the local event log while keeping language, audio and privacy preferences. It does not delete information already received by third-party services.
Removing application data or uninstalling may remove local files; operating-system backups may remain according to their settings.
We retain support correspondence for as long as needed to address the request and applicable responsibilities. Advertising and analytics providers retain information according to their purposes, service settings and policies.
You can request deletion by emailing us. We will explain which information we can identify and whether a request must also be directed to a provider. You do not need to create an account to contact us.
9. International transfers and security
Google, GameAnalytics and email providers may process information outside your country, including outside the European Economic Area. Applicable transfer safeguards are described in the providers' documentation and may include adequacy decisions or standard contractual clauses.
We limit information and access to what is needed for the purposes described. No storage or transmission system can guarantee absolute security.
10. Your rights
Depending on applicable law, you may request access, correction, deletion, restriction or portability of personal data, object to processing, and withdraw consent.
Contact blackholegamsstudios@gmail.com and identify the game as Black Hole Incremental. We may request proportionate information to locate the relevant records and verify your request. Because there are no player accounts, an email address alone may not identify gameplay records.
You may complain to your local data protection authority. In Spain, this is the Spanish Data Protection Agency (AEPD).

If you are a parent or guardian and believe that a child's personal information has been processed improperly, contact us so we can investigate and arrange deletion where appropriate.
